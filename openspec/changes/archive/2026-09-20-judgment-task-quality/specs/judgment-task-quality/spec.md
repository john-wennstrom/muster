## Purpose

Defines how a synthesized task list is assessed for semantic quality at plan time — verification strength, scope coverage, atomicity, dependency completeness, size, and requirement coverage — producing advisory findings for the user and the reviewer without altering any artifact or gate.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Tasks are assessed once per synthesis

After synthesis returns a task list that parses and validates, exactly one assessment SHALL cover all of its tasks together with the requirements and scenarios they implement. A task list of more than 40 tasks SHALL NOT be assessed.

#### Scenario: One assessment covers all tasks

- **WHEN** synthesis returns a valid task list of twelve tasks and judgment is available
- **THEN** one judgment request covers all twelve tasks and the requirements and scenarios

#### Scenario: Oversized task list is not assessed

- **WHEN** synthesis returns a valid task list of more than 40 tasks
- **THEN** no assessment is made and planning proceeds as it does without judgment

### Requirement: Findings are advisory and generated by code

Every finding SHALL be produced from a fixed template that names the task and the judged probability, and SHALL contain no text written by a model. Findings SHALL NOT modify any planning artifact, SHALL NOT set or imply a verdict, SHALL NOT be written to the review artifact, and SHALL NOT block or delay writing the artifacts.

#### Scenario: Findings use fixed templates

- **WHEN** a finding is produced
- **THEN** its text is one of the fixed templates filled with the task identifier and the judged probability

#### Scenario: Artifacts are written unchanged

- **WHEN** an assessment produces findings
- **THEN** the planning artifacts written are identical to those written without judgment and no review artifact is created or changed

### Requirement: Uncertain answers produce no finding

A question whose good answer is yes SHALL produce a finding when its probability is below 0.3, and a question whose good answer is no SHALL produce one when its probability is above 0.7. A task judged too large to verify as one unit at a score of at least 2.5 with confidence of at least 0.7 SHALL produce a finding. Coverage of the requirements SHALL produce a finding when its probability is below 0.3. Any other answer SHALL produce no finding.

#### Scenario: Confident concern produces a finding

- **WHEN** the verification-strength answer for a task is 0.1
- **THEN** a finding for that task is produced

#### Scenario: Uncertain answer produces none

- **WHEN** an answer lies between its thresholds
- **THEN** no finding is produced for it

### Requirement: Findings reach the user and the reviewer

In enforce mode the planning outcome SHALL state how many findings were produced and list them. The reviewer's prompt SHALL include the findings that are still current, marked as unverified automated notes for the reviewer to confirm or dismiss. Findings for task definitions that have since changed SHALL NOT be shown to the reviewer.

#### Scenario: Outcome lists findings

- **WHEN** an assessment in enforce mode produces findings
- **THEN** the planning outcome states the count and lists the findings

#### Scenario: Reviewer prompt includes current findings

- **WHEN** a review is requested and current findings exist
- **THEN** the reviewer's prompt includes them, marked as unverified, and the reviewer's verdict remains the reviewer's alone

#### Scenario: Stale findings are not shown

- **WHEN** the task definitions have changed since the assessment
- **THEN** the reviewer's prompt does not include that assessment's findings

### Requirement: Assessment never changes planning

For every unavailable reason, for a task list that does not parse or validate, and when judgment is disabled, the artifacts written and the planning outcome SHALL be identical to those without judgment. When judgment is disabled, no assessment work SHALL be performed.

#### Scenario: Every unavailable reason writes artifacts as today

- **WHEN** judgment is unavailable for any reason, including budget, timeout, invalid response, model mismatch, and a state too large to send
- **THEN** the artifacts and the planning outcome are identical to those without judgment

#### Scenario: Invalid task list is not assessed

- **WHEN** the synthesized task list fails to parse or validate
- **THEN** no assessment is made and the artifacts are written as they are without judgment

#### Scenario: Disabled judgment adds no work

- **WHEN** judgment is disabled
- **THEN** no request is sent, no record is written, and planning behaves as it does without judgment

### Requirement: Shadow mode records without surfacing

In shadow mode the findings SHALL be recorded and SHALL NOT appear in the planning outcome or the reviewer's prompt.

#### Scenario: Shadow findings are not surfaced

- **WHEN** an assessment in shadow mode produces findings
- **THEN** they are recorded, and neither the planning outcome nor a later reviewer prompt mentions them

### Requirement: Findings are reconciled with task outcomes

Each task's eventual outcome SHALL be recorded against whether it was flagged, keyed by a digest of the task definitions that ignores completion state. A report SHALL give, for each kind of finding, the rate at which flagged tasks failed to complete on their first attempt compared with the rate for tasks that were not flagged.

#### Scenario: Task outcome is recorded against findings

- **WHEN** a task's pipeline finishes after a plan-time assessment
- **THEN** the assessment's record holds the task's outcome and whether it was flagged, and ticking task checkboxes does not detach the record

#### Scenario: Report correlates findings with outcomes

- **WHEN** records for several changes are summarized
- **THEN** the report gives, for each finding kind, the non-completion rate for flagged and unflagged tasks

### Requirement: Assessment state is bounded and excerpted

The change summary in the state SHALL be at most 2,000 bytes, and the requirement and scenario text SHALL be excerpted so the state fits the judgment layer's limits. A state that is still too large SHALL be treated as unavailable.

#### Scenario: Long requirements are excerpted

- **WHEN** the requirement and scenario text is longer than the state allows
- **THEN** the state carries excerpts that keep every requirement and scenario name, and the assessment proceeds

### Requirement: Task quality egress is documented

The security documentation SHALL list, for this decision, the state that leaves the machine: the change summary excerpt, the requirement and scenario text, and each task's description, dependencies, scopes, and verification commands.

#### Scenario: Security documentation lists the task quality egress

- **WHEN** the security documentation's per-call-site table is read
- **THEN** it has a row for task quality naming exactly that state
